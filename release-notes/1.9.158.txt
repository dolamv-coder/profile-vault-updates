Each store now has its own slot limit: Target, Pokémon Center and Costco each start at 20, or at the number your seller already approved for you, now at every store. Submit shows each store's limit, and Request more slots asks for the number you need at each store. A Target store login no longer needs an inbox: leave the app password empty to save it without one. Costco and Pokémon Center logins still need theirs.
