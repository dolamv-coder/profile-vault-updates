Orders without a product picture now get one by themselves, Target pre-orders and Pokémon Center orders included: FAFO looks the item up in TCGplayer's catalog of Pokémon cards and products and puts its picture on every order of it, and on later orders of it too. Only a product with exactly the same name counts. A wrong one? Press Undo, or use Picture to change or remove it. Turn it off in Settings → Preferences → Find product pictures. On Generate, Copy on the selection bar (or right-click) copies the selected drafts' names, address line 1, address line 2 or cities, one per line.
