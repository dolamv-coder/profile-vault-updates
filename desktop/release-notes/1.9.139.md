This download opens on FAFO 1.9.139, the newest version. It's needed for one thing: **forwarding the store's emails** to a buyer you send orders to (Send to buyer on the Orders page). Type the buyer's email address there, and FAFO forwards every email about those orders (the confirmation, shipping, delivery, cancellation) from the inbox it came to, as they come, using the app password FAFO already has for that inbox. Older downloads can't send email.

Already using FAFO? It updates itself, so you only need this download to forward emails. To replace an older copy, close FAFO, then replace your old FAFO-win32-x64 folder (Orbit-win32-x64 on downloads before 1.9.76) with the one in this zip. Your vault, license and inboxes are kept.
