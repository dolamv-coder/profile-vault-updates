FAFO is Orbit's new name. This download opens on FAFO 1.9.76, the newest version. Only the name changed: your vault, license, settings and inboxes stay as they are.

Already using Orbit? It updates itself to the new name inside the app. This download also renames the app itself, so Windows shows FAFO in the Start menu, on the taskbar and on notifications. Close Orbit, extract this zip and open FAFO.exe in the FAFO-win32-x64 folder. Your vault, license and inboxes carry over, and your Orbit shortcuts switch to FAFO.
