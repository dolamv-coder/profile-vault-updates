This download opens on FAFO 1.9.136, the newest version. It's needed for one thing: **Find in Spam** on the Clean emails page, which searches your inboxes' Spam (for example for "about to ship" or an order number) and moves the emails you pick to your Inbox. It can also keep moving emails like those out of Spam every time FAFO syncs your email. Older downloads can't move emails out of Spam this way.

Already using FAFO? It updates itself, so you only need this download for Find in Spam. To replace an older copy, close FAFO, then replace your old FAFO-win32-x64 folder (Orbit-win32-x64 on downloads before 1.9.76) with the one in this zip. Your vault, license and inboxes are kept.
