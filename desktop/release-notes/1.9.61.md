Email sync and Clean emails now work on computers where antivirus email scanning (Avast, AVG, ESET, Kaspersky, Bitdefender and others), a VPN or a work network checks secure connections. Orbit trusts the same certificates Windows does, the way Chrome and Outlook do, instead of stopping at "The mail server's security certificate couldn't be verified." When a connection still can't be verified, Orbit names what's checking it, or says the computer's clock is off, and it never sends your password over a connection it can't verify.

Updating from an older download? Close Orbit, then replace your old Orbit-win32-x64 folder with the one in this zip. Your vault, license and inboxes are kept.
